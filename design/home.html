          The day strip

          The upright line is now. Each dot takes the colour of the agent it belongs to: a raised dot needs you, a solid dot is something for you, and a ring is coming up. Drag along the strip and Renoa shows you what happened or what's coming at that time.

          Renoa and your agents

          Tap Renoa to talk to her, or tap another agent to open its chat. While two agents talk, a packet runs the line between them in the asking agent's colour, and a line underneath says what they're talking about. Tap that line to read along.

          One thing at a time

          Renoa hands you one thing at a time from whoever needs you. Step through with the arrows or a swipe, and tap it to open that agent's chat. Agents fold several updates into one, so ten updates are never ten things to read.

          The bar at the bottom

          It takes the place of a tab bar. It shows who has written and what's next. Tap it and you land on your chats; swipe for routines, then for what's coming up this week. More pages can join later.

          Portraits

          Every agent gets a small drawn character the moment it's made, built from its name and what it does, in its own colour. Its eyes show how it's doing: they blink while it rests, look around while it works, and look up at you when it needs you. You can swap in your own picture or have one drawn. Renoa is the mascot, drawn once by us, and she wears the star. These faces are a sketch of the idea, not final art.

          Sign-up

          Three screens: your name, how a usual day goes, and meeting Renoa. Your answers start Renoa's picture of you; everything else it learns as you talk.
